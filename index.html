About

Grounded in systems, curious about models

My background sits at the seam between infrastructure and machine learning: I care as much about whether a model keeps working after it ships as I do about the accuracy it starts with.

I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science at Anna University in Chennai, then spent time as a software engineering intern at Gembrill Technologies, where I helped containerize ML microservices, build ETL pipelines, wire up CI/CD, and add production observability for models already in use. That work is what pulled me toward the data science and machine learning concentration I'm finishing now at Northeastern.
